## What changed

docs/auth/PROGRESS.md, docs/auth/FINDINGS.md, docs/auth/COMPONENTS.md, CHANGELOG.md,

Added references to AUTH_RULES.md in CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md.

## Why

Authentication is a security-critical workstream. Every later phase needs a shared,
unambiguous rulebook, a single place to track every checklist item to a file/test/status,
and a verifiable version history, so that no finding is lost, no status is overstated,
and every change is traceable.
